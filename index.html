Rithmatist Duel

Draw a circle first: it's your Line of Warding, and you stand inside it. Breach the other circle with waves (Lines of Vigor, at least 3 humps): the more curved the humps, the harder it hits circles and walls; the more spiky, the harder it hits chalklings. Straight lines (Lines of Forbiddance) are walls, up to 8 at a time. To make a chalkling, press Chalkling, then draw a chain from a green bind point, a circle on its end, your creature inside the circle (more detail = a stronger chalkling; rounder = more health, pointier = more bite, less chalk = faster), and a path out of it. Turn Chalkling off, press Eraser and click the chain: 3 seconds later it's gone and the chalkling comes alive.

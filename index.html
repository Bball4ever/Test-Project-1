Rithmatist Duel

An unofficial, non-commercial fan game based on Brandon Sanderson's The Rithmatist. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Brandon Sanderson or Dragonsteel Entertainment.

Draw a circle first: it's your Line of Warding, and you stand inside it. Breach the other circle with waves (Lines of Vigor, at least 3 humps): the more curved the humps, the harder it hits circles and walls; the more spiky, the harder it hits chalklings. Straight lines (Lines of Forbiddance) are walls, up to 8 at a time. To make a chalkling, press Chalkling, then draw a chain from a green bind point, a circle on its end, your creature inside the circle (more detail = a stronger chalkling; rounder = more health, pointier = more bite, less chalk = faster), and a path out of it. Turn Chalkling off, press Eraser and click the chain: 3 seconds later it's gone and the chalkling comes alive.

Seating:

Two people on one touchscreen, the rest bots. Side by side: you both sit at the bottom edge. Facing each other: one of you sits at the top edge, and that player's buttons and name are turned round to face them. Each stroke belongs to whoever's area it starts in.

Your team (cool colours) against the other team (warm colours); everyone else is a bot. Teammates' waves pass through each other's circles and chalklings, but walls stop everyone's. If your circle breaks you're out, but your team plays on: the last team standing wins.

Rithmatist Duel

Draw a circle first: it's your Line of Warding, and you stand inside it. Breach the other circle with waves (Lines of Vigor). Straight lines (Lines of Forbiddance) are walls. To make a chalkling, press Chalkling, then draw a chain from a green bind point, a circle on its end, your creature inside the circle, and a path out of it. Turn Chalkling off, press Eraser and click the chain: 3 seconds later it's gone and the chalkling comes alive.
